# Figma AI - Intelligent Design Assistant for the Figma Editor

## About Figma AI

Figma AI is a set of artificial intelligence tools built directly into the Figma editor. It is designed to accelerate and simplify the work of designers by removing routine tasks from their daily process. Instead of switching between separate services, designers can use Figma AI design tools right where the work already happens. Figma AI plugin functionality keeps everything inside a familiar environment, while Figma AI features cover common design actions such as generating layouts, adjusting components, and refining visual details. Teams that rely on Figma AI design tools often notice that small repetitive steps disappear first, which leaves more room for creative decisions. Figma AI workflow support means the assistant follows the natural rhythm of a project rather than forcing a new one. Whether you are building a landing page, a mobile screen, or a design system, Figma AI features help you move from an idea to a usable result with less friction.
